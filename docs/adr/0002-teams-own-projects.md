# Teams own projects; anyone may log on any project

A project belongs to exactly one team. Every user in the workspace can see and log on every project; teams grant ownership and reporting rights, not access. A lead reads all hours logged on their team's projects, including hours logged by people from other teams.

Why: helping another team for a day is normal work, and per-project access lists would make the common case an admin task. Ownership still answers "who maintains this project" and "whose numbers are these".

Consequence: a lead sees the names and hours of colleagues outside their team whenever those colleagues work on the team's projects. That is intended, and it is why project ownership has to be assigned deliberately.
